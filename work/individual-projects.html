Solo Work

Individual projects, along the years

A little bit Burton, a lot of me: projects I designed, built, and saw through on my own, outside of internships and team briefs.

I'm a creative person before anything else, and I like bringing my own perspective into what I make. Tim Burton's visual storytelling has been a big influence on my style: playful, atmospheric, and always with a bit of narrative behind it. These are a few of the projects where that came through most, since there was no client or team brief shaping the direction, just me.

Gamifying MyFitnessPal

A gamification concept built on top of an already existing app, MyFitnessPal. The full explanation is in the video.

Mindfulness App

A mindfulness app I designed from the ground up. Again, the video speaks for itself.

Multimodal Finger-Counting App

A multimodal interaction prototype that tracks your fingers through the camera, counts them, and awards points after three correct interactions in a row. It was built for children, to help them learn to count to five.

Sound Design: The Last of Us

I remade and edited the sound design for a trailer of The Last of Us using Ableton, rebuilding the soundscape from scratch.
