Studying Abroad

Design across borders

Two international experiences that pushed my design and communication thinking outside a single cultural context.

🇸🇪 Jönköping University, Sweden

Minor Abroad — Media and Communication · August 2024 – January 2025

I spent a semester at Jönköping University on a Media and Communication minor, studying how media, culture, and communication intersect in a Scandinavian context. It sharpened how I think about audience, tone, and message — skills that carry directly into UX writing and content design.

🇸🇪 Add photos or coursework from Jönköping here

🇨🇦 Humber College, Toronto, Canada

Summer School — Social Media Marketing · Summer 2025

During a summer school at Humber College, I worked on a social media marketing project for the Toronto Islands, developing content and campaign strategy to promote the islands as a destination. It was a hands-on crash course in marketing a real place to a real audience, on a tight timeline.

🏝️ Add Toronto Islands campaign work here

Why it matters: designing for people outside your own culture forces you to slow down and question your assumptions — a habit that's shaped how I approach every project since, at home or abroad.
