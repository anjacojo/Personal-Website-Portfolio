Cinematography & sound design

Where storytelling turns into image and atmosphere — my video and sound work, from documentary to mood pieces.

How I approach it

Cinematography and sound design are where my storytelling instincts get the most literal outlet. Whether it's a short documentary or a mood-driven edit, I think about pacing, framing, and sound the same way I think about a user flow — what does the viewer feel, and when?

This includes the short documentary-style video I produced during my internship at DLL, capturing a company event through a more cinematic lens than the usual corporate recap — as well as personal sound design experiments exploring atmosphere and tension.
