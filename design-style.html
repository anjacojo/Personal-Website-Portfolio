Personal Style

A little bit Burton, a lot of me

This whole site is basically an extension of this page — so consider it the mood board behind everything else here.

Where my style comes from

I'm a creative person before anything else — I don't really know how to keep my personal taste out of my work, and I've stopped trying to. If you look closely at what I make, you'll notice I'm very drawn to Tim Burton's visual world: a bit dark, a bit whimsical, a little crooked around the edges, always with a story hiding underneath.

That shows up as high-contrast palettes, slightly off-kilter shapes instead of perfectly straight ones, and a soft spot for turning something functional (like an e-learning module, or this very website) into something with a bit of theatre to it.

I love working in teams, and I've noticed the bigger and messier the project, the more room there is for this kind of imagination to actually show up in the final result.

What defines it

  • Mood over minimalism — I'd rather a design feel like something than say nothing at all.
  • Playful asymmetry — a little imperfection, on purpose.
  • Storytelling first — every project needs a "why," even the practical ones.
  • Color as character — purples, deep contrasts, and a warm accent to keep it from getting too gloomy.
