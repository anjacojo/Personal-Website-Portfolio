About Me

A little bit gothic, a little bit gamified, all creative.

I'm a UX/UI & graphic designer with 4+ years of experience across Learning & Development, communications, and marketing. I hold a Bachelor's in Creative Media and Game Technologies (Designer track) from Saxion University of Applied Sciences.

I'm a creative person at heart — I always blend imagination with my work, and if you look closely you'll see I'm very drawn to Tim Burton's style: a little dark, a little whimsical, always a bit of a story behind it. I love working in teams, and I think the bigger the project, the more room there is for my creativity and imagination to show.

Along the way I've picked up some international perspective too — a semester abroad in Jönköping, Sweden, and a summer school in Toronto, Canada — both of which shaped how I think about communication and design across cultures.
